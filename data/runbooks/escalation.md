# Escalation and approval

## Escalate when evidence is uncertain or risk is high

Send the incident, affected run, ranked hypotheses, evidence references, and proposed action to the simulated data-engineering review queue. State what evidence is missing and what decision is required.

## Approval record

High-risk or ambiguous repairs require an explicit approval decision before execution. Record reviewer role, decision, reason, timestamp, and action proposal. A dashboard button or model recommendation is not approval by itself.
